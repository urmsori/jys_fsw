# pbs_fsw

product breakdown structure for fsw

## purpose

define product components and deliverables

## approach

- top-down decomposition of products
- hierarchical component organization
- physical and logical breakdown

## structure

- level 1: major product components
- level 2: sub-components and modules
- level n: smallest deliverable units

## principles

- product-oriented decomposition
- physical and logical components
- clear component boundaries
- traceable to requirements
